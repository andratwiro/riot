# RIOT · Reus demo session — moderator notes

A 10-card showcase deck for a live demo. Curated for the cards a citizen actually
leans into — vivid, surprising, or genuinely divisive — rather than the most
evenly-split votes. The deck lives in code as `CITY_CONFIG.demo_deck`
(`cities/reus/config.js`) and is pinned to the top of the moderator's session
picker as **DEMO · the curated showcase deck**.

## How to run it

1. Open the viewer as moderator: append `?role=moderator` to the URL
   (e.g. `index.html?role=moderator`). Reus is the default city.
2. In the session picker, choose **DEMO** (pinned first).
3. Trim/reorder if you like, set the clock, and start. Everyone gets the same
   set; order is per person. The moderator screen is the projector/stage view.

The room votes each card blind, then the reveal shows where the room and the
elected councillors split. Remember the booth doctrine: no valence before the
vote — let people commit first.

## The deck (run order)

Order is a build: grabber → light laughs → public-money eyebrow-raisers →
identity/rights flashpoints last, to close strong. Vote shown as
**for–against–abstain** (the councillors' real result).

### 1. 16 people lived in the hospital car park — `PLE_10_2025_ORD-p11`
*Vox · rejected · 2–23–2 · Seguretat · 2025-11-21*
A group of 16 people lived for about a month and a half in the underground car
park of Hospital Sant Joan (run by the municipal company Reus Mobilitat). Vox
demanded reports within 30 days, more cameras, and disciplinary files.
**Why it grabs:** the image alone stops the room. Great cold open.

### 2. Where dogs can (and can't) pee — `PLE_10_2025_ORD-p8`
*govern · approved · 15–4–8 · Medi ambient · 2025-11-21*
The final approval of the new waste & street-cleaning ordinance — duties, bans
and fines — which literally regulates where dogs may relieve themselves.
**Why it grabs:** a dry "ordinance" hiding a line everyone has an opinion on.

### 3. Free tampons & pads in municipal buildings — `PLE_08_2025_ORD-p12`
*CUP · approved · 24–1–1 · Drets i igualtat · 2025-09-19*
Study installing free menstrual-product dispensers in civic centres, libraries
and sports halls — and at big city events.
**Why it grabs:** relatable, mildly provocative, splits a room cleanly.

### 4. 6 parking spots scrapped so a Mercadona fits its lifts — `PLE_9_2023_ORD-p13`
*govern · approved · 25–2–0 · Mobilitat · 2023-07-21*
Removing 6 spaces and 4 storage units from the public Pallol car park to fit the
lifts of a new Mercadona.
**Why it grabs:** tiny line item, big "of course Mercadona gets its way" reaction.

### 5. A new mayor's-office confidence job, up to €56,408/yr — `PLE_9_2023_ORD-p5`
*govern · approved · 20–7–0 · Govern i institucional · 2023-07-21*
Create a new freely-appointed "personal de confiança" post.
**Why it grabs:** patronage-job outrage bait; pairs well with the budget talk.

### 6. 22,700 m² of public land to a private art foundation, 50 years, no tender — `PLE_2_2026_ORD-p6`
*govern · approved · 24–2–1 · Cultura i esport · 2026-02-20*
Direct award (no public tender) of two municipal plots to a private non-profit
art foundation for 50 years to build an open-air exhibition space.
**Why it grabs:** public land + no competition — instant "wait, what?".

### 7. The "cursed" €11M Hispània car park — mercury & lead in the soil — `PLE_9_2023_ORD-p16`
*CUP · rejected · 2–25–0 · Mobilitat · 2023-07-21*
CUP wanted to stop the €11M underground car park (mercury and lead found in the
soil) and turn it into a park.
**Why it grabs:** pollution + money + cars-vs-green. Companion card if you want
the sequel: `PLE_09_2025_ORD-p31` (the city later defers repaying the €6M loan).

### 8. Tie a shop's opening licence to Catalan signage — `PLE_9_2023_ORD-p14`
*Junts · approved · 22–3–2 · Drets i igualtat · 2023-07-21*
A "Pacte Local pel català" that would link a business's opening licence to
labelling in Catalan.
**Why it grabs:** language politics that lands hard locally. Companion: the 2025
follow-through `PLE_08_2025_ORD-p13` ("A Reus, en català!").

### 9. A registry of police ID-checks to detect racist bias — `PLE_11_2025_ORD-p12`
*CUP · rejected · 2–23–2 · Drets i igualtat · 2025-12-19*
A protocol and municipal registry of Guàrdia Urbana stops to detect racial
profiling, with independent oversight and mandatory anti-racism training.
**Why it grabs:** policing + race + oversight; almost the whole chamber voted no.

### 10. Vox: scrap the Morocco-funded Arabic program in 5 schools — `PLE_08_2025_ORD-p11`
*Vox · rejected · 1–25–1 · Cultura i esport · 2025-09-19*
Ask the Generalitat to end the PLACM (Arabic language & Moroccan culture),
taught by Morocco-paid teachers in 5 Reus schools.
**Why it grabs:** the clearest identity/immigration flashpoint — close on this.
